Understanding Your Bail Options

Cash Bonds vs. Surety Bonds Comparison:

Feature Cash Bond Surety Bond (24/7 Bail Agency)
Payment Amount You must pay 100% of the full bail amount directly to the court. You pay a small percentage (typically 10%, or special rates) to our licensed agent.
Upfront Cash Very high (e.g., $10,000 bail requires $10,000 cash upfront). Affordable (e.g., $10,000 bail requires only $1,000 or less to the bondsman).
Convenience Requires cash, cashier's check, or money order in person. Bond approval by phone, credit cards accepted, .
Refundability Refunded by court (minus fees) after case closure if all dates are met. Fee is non-refundable, paying for immediate release and financial guarantee.
