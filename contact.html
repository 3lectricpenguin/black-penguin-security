Guidelines for Calling

  • 1.
    Stay Calm: We know this is a stressful situation, but we are here to guide you and handle all jail logistics.
  • 2.
    Have Information Ready: Let us know the defendant's full name, date of birth, and the jail they are currently held in (if known).
  • 3.
    Ask Questions: Our licensed agents are happy to explain the charges, bond amounts, and customized payment plans.
  • 4.
    Confirm Co-signer Eligibility: Be prepared to discuss employment, residency, or collateral details to secure the bond.
