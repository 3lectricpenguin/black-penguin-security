## Guidelines for Calling
To help us secure a fast release, please try to follow these guidelines when you call:
1.  **Stay Calm:** We know this is a stressful situation, but we are here to guide you and handle the details.
2.  **Have Information Ready:** Let us know the defendant's full name, date of birth, and the jail they are currently held in (if known).
3.  **Ask Questions:** Our agents are happy to explain the charges, bond amounts, and payment plans.
4.  **Confirm Co-signer Eligibility:** Be prepared to discuss employment, residency, or collateral details to secure the bond.
