## How to Get Started in 3 Easy Steps

Getting your loved one home is simple. Just follow these three steps:

### 1. Call Us at 1-866-322-2245
Reach out to our team immediately. To speed up the process, please try to have the defendant’s full name, date of birth, and the jail where they are being held ready. If you don't have all this information, don't worry—our agents can help look it up.

### 2. Customize Your Payment Plan
We believe that financial constraints shouldn't prevent you from helping your family. We offer flexible, customized payment plans tailored to your financial situation. We will finalize the simple paperwork quickly.

### 3. Fast Release Process
Once the paperwork is finalized and payment is arranged, our licensed bail agent will immediately post the bond at the jail. We work diligently to secure the fastest release possible.
