## Specialized Bond Offerings for Legal Practices

### Federal Bonds & Nebbia Hearings
Federal bonds require rigorous documentation and a clear source of funds. We have over 30 years of experience coordinating with defense attorneys to prepare packages for Nebbia hearings, ensuring that the court accepts the bond quickly.

### Large Bond Underwriting
We have the financial capacity and experience to write high-value bonds. Our senior underwriters work efficiently to structure surety bonds for major felony cases, minimizing delays for high-profile or complex matters.

### Out-of-County & Multi-State Bonds
If your client is detained in another county or state, our network allows us to post bonds remotely. We cover 12 counties in Michigan and 9 counties in Ohio directly, with regional support beyond.
