1

Reach out to our team immediately. To speed up the process, please try to have the defendant’s full name, date of birth, and the jail where they are being held ready. If you don't have all this information, don't worry—our agents will help look it up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the release process take?

Release times vary by jail capacity and staff, but our agents post bond immediately to expedite release. It usually takes 1 to 4 hours.

What is the cost?

By law, the premium is typically 10% of the full bond amount. We offer competitive rates and flexible payment options to fit your budget.

Do I need a co-signer?

Most bail bonds require a co-signer (indemnitor) to guarantee the defendant appears in court. Learn more on our How Bail Works page.

Need Help Right Now?

Speak directly to a licensed agent instantly. No machines, no long hold times. We are ready to help you post bail immediately.
